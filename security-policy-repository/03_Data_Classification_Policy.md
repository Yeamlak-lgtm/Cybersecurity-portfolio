# Data Classification Policy

## Purpose
The purpose of this Data Classification Policy is to establish a framework for classifying and protecting information based on its sensitivity and business value.
Proper classification helps ensure that company and customer information is handled, stored, transmitted, and disposed of securely.

---
## Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees
- All Contractors
- Third-party users with authorized acceess
- All company information in pyhsical and electronic formats.

---
## Policy Statement
All information created, stored, processed, or transmitted by Ariel Financial Services must be classified according to its level of sensitivity.
Users are responsible for handling informatioin according to its assigned classification.

---
## Data Classification Levels
Public
- Information approved for public release.
Examples:
- Company website contents
- Marketing materials
- Public announcements

Internals
- Information intended for internal business use.
Examples:
- Internal procedures
- Employee directories
- Meeting notes

Confidential
- Sensitive business information that required retricted access.
Examples:
- Financial records
- Employeee records
- Vendor contracts

Restricted
- The organization's most sensetive information requiring the highest level of protection.
Examples:
- Customer financial information
- PErsonally identifiable information (PII)
- Authentication credintials
- Encryption keys

---
## Responsibilities
Employees are responsible for properly classifying and protecting information.
Managers are responsible for ensuring their teams follow this policy.
The Information Security Team is responsible for maintaining classification standards and providing guidance.

---
## Compliance
Failure to comply may result in disciplinary action and could increase organizational risk.

---
## Exceptions
Exception must be approved by the Information Security Team and documented with appropriate business justification.

---
## Review Schedule
This policy will be reviewed annnually or whenever significant business, technology, or regulatory changes occur.
