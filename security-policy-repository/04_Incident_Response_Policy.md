## Incident Response Policy
# Purpose
The purpose of this Incident Response Policy is to establish a structured approach for identifying, reporting, responding to, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents.
This policy helps minimize the impact of security incidents and supports the timely restoration os business operations.

---
## Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees
- Contractors
- Third-party users with authorized access
- All company-owned systems, devices, applications, and networks

---
## Policy Statement
All suspected  or confirmed cybersecurity incidents must be reported immediately to the Information Security Team. 
The organization will investigate, contain, eradicate, and recover from incidents using a consistent and documented response process.

---
## Incident Categories
Examples of cybersecurity incidents include:
- Phishing attacks
- Malware or ransomware infections
- Unauthorized access
- Data breaches
- Lost or stolen company devices
- Denial-of-service (DoS) attacks

---
## Reporting Requirements
Employees must:
- Report suspected incidents immediately.
- Preserve any evidence related to the incident.
- Avoid attempting to investigate or resolve incidents without authorization.

---
## Incident Response Process
The Information Security Team will:
1. Identify the incident
2. Contain the threat
3. Eradicate the root cause.
4. Recover affected systems.
5. Document lessons learned and implement improvements.

---
## Role and Responsibilities
Employees are responsible for reporting suspected incidents promptly.
Managers are responsible for supporting response activities within their departments.
The Information Security Team is responsible for coordinating incident response, communication, and recovery efforts. 

---
## Compliance 
Failure to report cybersecurity incidents or comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action.

---
## Exceptions
Exceptions must be approved by the Information Security Team and documented with appropriate business justification.

---
## Review Schedule
This policy will be reviewed annually or whenever significant business, technology, or regulatory changes occur.
