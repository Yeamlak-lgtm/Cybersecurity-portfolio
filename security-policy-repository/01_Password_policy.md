# Password Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this password is to establish requirements for creating, managing, and protecting passwords used to access Ariel Financial Services' information systems and business applications.
Strong password practices help reduce the risk of unauthorized access, account compromise, and data breaches.

---

## Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees
- Contractors
- interns
- Third-party users with authorized access
- All company-owned systems and applications.

## Policy Statement
All users are responsible for protecting their authentication credentials.
Passwords must be  created and maintained in accordance with this policy to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of company information.

---

### Password Requirements
Employees must:
- Create passwords containing at least 14 characters.
- Use a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Use unique passwords for company accounts.
- Change passwords immediately if compromise is suspected.
- Never share passwords with other individuals.
Employees must not:
- Reuse previously compromised passwords.
- Store passwords in unsecured documents.
- Share passwords through email, applications, or phone calls.

---
## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Multi-Factor authentication (MFA) is required for:
- Microsoft 365
- Remote access
- Administrative accounts
- Cloud services
- Any system containing sensitive company or customer information

---
## Responsibilities
Employees are responsible for complying with this policy and protecting their authentication credentials.
Managers are responsible for ensuring employees understand and follow this policy.
The Information Security Team is responsible for monitoring compliance and maintaining authentication standards.

---
## Compliance
Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action and, where appropriate, revocation of system access.

---
## Exceptions
Exceptions to this policy must be approved by the Information Security Team and documented with appropriate business justification.

---
## Review Schedule
This policy will be reviewed annually or whenever significant changes occur to business operations, technology, or regulatory requirements.
