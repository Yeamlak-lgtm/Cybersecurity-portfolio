## Remote Work Policy
Purpose
The purpose of this Remote Work Policy is to establish cybersecurity requirements for employees working remotely to protect company information, systems, and technology resources. 
This policy helps reduce cybersecurity risks associated with remote access and supports secure business operations outside of company facilities.

---
## Scope
This policy applies to:
- All employees approved for remote work.
- Contractors with remote access.
- Company-owned and authorized personal devices used for business purposes.

---
## Policy Statement
Employees working remotely must maintain the same level of security as when working from company offices. 
Remote work must be conducted using approved devices, secure connections, and company-authorized applications.

---
## Remote Work Requirements
Employees must:
- Use company-approved devices whenever possible.
- Connect to company resources using secure, approved methods.
- Lock devices when unattended.
- Keep operating systems and software up to date.
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately.

---
## Protection of Company Information
Employees must:
- Protect confidential company information from unauthorized access.
- Avoid discussing sensitive information in public places.
- Store company information only on approved systems and cloud services.
- Dispose of sensitive documents securely.

 ---
 ## Network Security
 Employees must:
 - Use secure, password-protected Wi-Fi networks.
 - Avoid conducting company business on public Wi-Fi unless using approved secure access methods.
 - Ensure home networks are protected with strong passwords.

---
## Responsibilites
Employees are responsible for following this policy while working remotely.
Managers are responsible for ensuring remote employees understand and comply with security requirements.
The Information Security Team is responsible for maintaining secure remote access standards and providing security guidance. 

---
## Compliance
Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action and revocation of remote work privileges.

---
### Exceptions 
Exceptions must be approved by the Information Security Team and documented with appropriate business justification.

---
## Review Schedule
This policy will be reviewed annually or whenever significant business, technology, or regulatory changes occur.
