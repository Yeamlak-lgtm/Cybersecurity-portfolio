# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose
The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy is to define the appropriate use of Ariel Financial Services' information systems, technology resources, and company data.
This policy helps protect organizational assets, maintain cybersecurity, and ensure technology resources are used responsibly. 

---
## Scope
This policy applies to :
- All employees
- Contractors
- Interns
- Third-party users with authorized access
- All company-owned devices, systems, applications, and networks

---
## Policy Statement
Company technology resources are provided for authorized business purposes.
Users are expected to use these resources responsibly, ethically, and in accordance with company policies.

---
Acceptable Use Requirements
Users are expected to: 
- Use company devices primarily for business purposes.
- Protect company information from unauthorized acsess.
- Lock devices when unattended.
- Report lost or stolen devices immediately.
- Follow all cybersecurity policies and procedures.

---
## Prohibited Activities
Users must not:
- Install unauthorized software.
- Share company credentials with others.
- Access illegal, offensive, or malicious websites using company devices.
- Disable security software or system protections.
- Download or distribute unauthorized copyrighted material.
- Connect unauthorized devices to the company network.

---
## Email and Internet Usage
Employees must:
- Exercise caution when opening email attachments or clicking links.
- Use company email professionally.
- Avoid transmitting confidential information through unsecured communication channels.

---
## Monitoring
Ariel Financial Services reserves the right to monitor the use of company systems, networks, email, and devices to protect business operations and ensure compliance with company policies.
Users should not expect privacy when using company-owned technology resources.

---
## Responsibilities
Employees are responsible for complying with this policy.
Managers are responsible for promoting responsible technology use.
The Information Security Team is responsible for monitoring compliance and investigating policy violations.

---
## Compliance
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, suspension of system access, or other appropriate actions in accordance with company procedures.

---
## Exceptions
Exceptions must be approved by the Information Security Team and documented with appropriate business justification.

---
## Review Schedule
This policy will be reviewed annually or whenever significant business, technology, or regulatory changes occur.
